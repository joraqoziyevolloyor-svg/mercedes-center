2. Everything else is our own work (39-odam2/src/build_people.py, people.json, clips.py, sheet.py):
   body shaping and face variation, hair volumes (parametric lofts, no external hair assets), garments (offset shells
   with loose hems and fold noise, hoodie hood loft), sneakers (parametric sole + upper lofts), the 21-bone skeleton and
   automatic weights, all textures (procedural: value noise painted in numpy from baked position/normal/material-id
   maps — colour atlas, roughness, tangent-space normal map, eye texture), and the 14 animation clips (pp_anim.json,
   authored procedurally in clips.py on the m1 rig — identical to 28-odam/models/pp_anim.json).

project without restriction.
